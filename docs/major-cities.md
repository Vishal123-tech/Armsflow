Major city layer

Source: work/ne_capital_source.geojson, Natural Earth populated places simple, retrieved 2026-11-07. Public domain. Coordinates and names are used as provided.

Selection: source worldcity=1 OR source pop_max >=1,000,000 OR among the two largest source cities in its country with pop_max >=100,000. Capital-layer records are excluded by name/country pair. Source population figures are historical estimates, used only for display selection, not shown as current population. This is not a measured political, economic or military power ranking.

Gold capitals appear at 2.5x; teal world cities and cities with at least 5 million source population appear at 3.5x; remaining selected cities at 5x. Capital labels have priority; overlap suppression reveals additional labels with closer zoom.
